# Enterprise Service Operations Runbook

## Purpose

Operate the ESMP catalog, customer-success workflows, administrative controls,
operational intelligence, and improvement backlog without bypassing certification.

## Procedure

1. Validate all ten required catalog services have owners, SLAs, versions,
   dependencies, health evidence, and certification state.
2. Refresh tenant-scoped customer success and operational metrics.
3. Reject invalid evidence and prevent cross-tenant dashboard projection.
4. Record every administrative mutation through the governance audit sink.
5. Classify observations into the governed improvement backlog.
6. Require evidence, analysis, prioritization, approval, implementation,
   certification, deployment, measurement, and knowledge capture in order.
7. Store generated operational evidence through the certification repository.
8. Halt promotion if any mandatory ESMP gate is missing or failed.

AI may summarize trends, identify recurrence, draft runbook changes, and recommend
priorities. A human must review the output; AI cannot execute privileged actions
or bypass certification.
