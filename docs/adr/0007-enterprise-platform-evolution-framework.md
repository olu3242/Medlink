# ADR 0007: Enterprise Platform Evolution Framework

- Status: Accepted, inactive pending RC1 GA
- Date: 2026-07-30

## Decision

Engines 31–35 govern long-term portfolio investment, architecture integrity,
AI assets, ecosystem trust, and advisory simulation. They extend existing
governance, platform, AI, integration, analytics, and certification ownership.

The framework can be certified in source while remaining inactive. Production
activation requires separate, valid RC1 General Availability evidence. Digital
twin output and AI recommendations have no production mutation authority.

## Consequences

- No healthcare workflow or duplicate runtime is introduced.
- Architectural drift and partner trust failures produce fail-closed results.
- Models require versioning, evaluation, approval, certification, monitoring,
  and rollback controls.
- EPEF remains inactive until RC1 GA.
